Privacy Policy

How we handle your data with care and respect.

What We Collect

When you submit a story or contact us, we collect the information you provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address (if provided)
  • The content of your story or message

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only for the following purposes:

  • To publish your story on the website (if approved)
  • To respond to your messages
  • To send moderation notifications

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Storage

Stories are stored in Cloudflare KV storage. Email addresses are stored only as part of story records. If you contact us, your message is forwarded via email and not stored on our servers after processing.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your story or personal data at any time by contacting us at rafaabou@proton.me.

Cookies

This site uses localStorage to remember your language and theme preferences. No tracking cookies are used. YouTube embeds may set cookies when you play videos.
